# Code of Conduct

## Scope and expectations

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where people participate in or represent the project.
Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

- Discuss evidence and code, not a contributor's worth or identity.
- Give constructive, specific feedback; accept corrections and differing views.
- Respect people's time, accessibility needs, boundaries, and privacy.
- Credit others' work and represent affiliations, results, and limitations honestly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, targeted insults,
publication of private information without consent, and retaliation against
reporters or participants are not acceptable. Repeated unwanted contact and
attempts to evade moderation are also prohibited.

## Reporting and response

For sensitive reports, use the official
[PyMC Labs contact page](https://www.pymc-labs.com/contact), identify this project,
and request private contact with an uninvolved maintainer. Do not put personal
information or sensitive allegations into a public issue. If a report involves
the person handling it, request review by another PyMC Labs maintainer.

Maintainers should consider context and impact, limit sharing to people needed
to handle the report, and protect reporters from retaliation. Depending on the
conduct, responses may include requesting a correction, removing content,
issuing a warning, limiting participation temporarily, or excluding a participant
from project spaces. Serious threats may require immediate action.

A participant may request reconsideration through the same private channel and
ask for an uninvolved reviewer. Maintainers are accountable to these standards
too; moderation should be proportionate and explained to affected participants
where doing so does not compromise another person's privacy or safety.
